## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Lets play FIFA worldcup in these boots - Are You Ready!
Nano Banana Pro on @higgsfield
Prompt and Use:
A dramatic worm's eye view of [PLAYER NAME] standing powerfully with one foot slightly forward, holding a perfectly square-shaped football in both hands at waist level, wearing extravagant oversized golden football shoes with extremely long curved pointed noses/toes dramatically extending forward, highly detailed shiny metallic gold texture with luxurious reflections, shoes dominate the lower half of the frame with massive exaggerated scale, square football clearly visible in mid-frame, [PLAYER DESCRIPTION] above, pure white seamless background, clean studio lighting, ultra sharp focus on golden shoes and square ball, cinematic, 9:16
How to Use the Template
Replace the placeholders:[PLAYER NAME] → e.g. Lionel Messi, Erling Haaland, Kylian Mbappé or you can use your reference image as well
[PLAYER DESCRIPTION] → Short description of the player’s build, pose, or expression (this is key for good results)
````
