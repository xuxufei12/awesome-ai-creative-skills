## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Premium single-origin coffee commercial for [brand] espresso. 0-2s: top-down macro of crema blooming in a white porcelain demitasse, hazelnut tiger-stripe marbling swirling outward, steam rising in slow translucent ribbons. 2-4s: camera tilts up and pushes in on a barista's hand lowering the cup onto a brushed-walnut counter, warm window light from frame-left, dust motes drifting through the beam. 4-6s: a slow 45-degree arc reveals dark-roasted beans scattered beside the saucer, shallow depth of field racking focus from the beans to the cup rim. 6-8s: final lock-off as a single thread of steam crosses the brand mark stamped into the saucer. The cup, crema marbling, and logo stay consistent throughout — no melting, ghosting, warping, extra fingers, or artifacts. Golden warmth, ceramic-and-steam texture, faint cup clink and a soft trailing pour.
````
