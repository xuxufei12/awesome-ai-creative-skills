## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Horizontal cinematic before-after ad. SUBJECT: footwear — battered old sneakers replaced by [product] water-repellent runners. 0–2s (low side-tracking macro beside the foot, dull overcast light, gritty texture): a worn sneaker on wet pavement, sole peeling, gray water seeping through a split seam as it flexes mid-step. 2–3s (hard whip-pan interrupt, a swoosh of displaced air): motion blur smears across the frame. 3–7s (same tracking stride, now in [product], one droplet in slow motion): a water bead strikes the toecap and rolls off, catching a low golden sun, the foot landing clean and sure on the same wet street. 7–10s (dolly pulls wide): a confident full-stride silhouette against the glistening road. FIDELITY: the sneaker holds one consistent shape, colorway, eyelet count, and stitching pattern across beats — no deformation, sole morphing, extra eyelets, or logo drift. SOUND: wet squelch, the whip-pan whoosh, a crisp confident footfall.
````
