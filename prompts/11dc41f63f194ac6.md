## Prompt / 提示词

````text
An effortless, sun-baked moment of cool on a coastal boardwalk at high noon. 0-2s: a shimmering heat-haze fills the frame as a man pushes [brand] sunglasses up the bridge of his nose with one finger; the bleached planks and a pale sea blur behind him, lens flare skating across the top of frame. 2-4s: a smooth 180-degree orbit around him as sea breeze lifts his collar, the tinted lenses mirroring rolling surf and a thin chrome horizon, his expression unbothered and assured. 4-6s: cut to a tight macro of the frames — sculptural acetate gleaming, sunlight raking the temple arms — then pull to a wide as he turns to lean on a sun-bleached railing facing the water. The sunglasses keep a consistent frame shape, hinge, and lens tint throughout; no warping, reflection glitches, drift, or artifacts. Audio: a mellow downtempo beat, gulls, soft surf, wind across the mic. Mood: elevated, breezy, aspirational cool. Palette: bleached sand, sun-faded blue, hot white highlights.
````
