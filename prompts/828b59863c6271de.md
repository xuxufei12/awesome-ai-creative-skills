## Prompt / 提示词

````text
“Creative composite World Cup poster, 3:4 vertical aspect ratio, clean white background, studio lighting, sharp focus. A real-life photograph of a [NATION] flag is physically placed and seamlessly integrated with a hand-drawn black ink cartoon illustration. The drawing is simple, expressive line art with minimal shading — like a quick sketch on paper. A cartoon fan is on their knees holding the flag above their head with both hands stretched high, head tilted back screaming to the sky in pure euphoria, tears streaming down their face. At the top, write the motto/slogan associated with the football team “TEAM CHANT/MOTTO” in clean sans-serif. At the bottom, write the nation name “TEAM” in clean bold font. Photorealistic flag + bold black doodle art, perfect seamless blend, witty scroll-stopping poster aesthetic.”
````
