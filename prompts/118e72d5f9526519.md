## Prompt / 提示词

````text
A candid, professional editorial photograph of [NAME] standing indoors in front of a dark wooden-framed glass window. She has long, slightly messy hair with a stray strand at the top, falling naturally over her shoulders. She has a soft, sleepy expression, gently rubbing her half-closed right eye with her left hand as if she just woke up.
She is wearing a white long-sleeved off-the-shoulder cropped top that slants down on the right side, exposing her shoulder and abdomen, paired with blue dolphin-style athletic shorts featuring white side stripes and a drawstring waist. Her posture is relaxed and casually leaning. In her right hand, she holds a white ceramic mug with a stylized blue cat logo, from which subtle steam rises. Part of a dark wood counter surface is visible in the lower left foreground.
Through the glass window behind her, a sunlit green forest with lush foliage and light filtering through the trees is visible in a soft bokeh. Bright, natural morning light streams through the window, creating a soft backlight that highlights the contours of her hair and natural skin texture.
Medium shot, eye-level angle, shot on an 85mm f/1.8 lens with a shallow depth of field. Ultra-detailed, realistic physical rendering of light and fabrics, crisp focus on the subject, photorealistic HDR quality, 9:16 aspect ratio.
````
