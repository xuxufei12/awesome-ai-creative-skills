## Prompt / 提示词

````text
"Ultra-expressive mixed-media portrait style that fuses sculptural impasto oil painting with refined editorial sketch illustration. Built from thousands of thick palette-knife strokes, fragmented paint marks, and energetic hand-drawn cross-hatching. Semi-abstract realism where subjects emerge through a sophisticated combination of textured oil-paint buildup, expressive ink linework, architectural sketch marks, and loose watercolor washes. Rich tactile surface featuring layered pigment accumulation, visible directional brushstrokes, graphite textures, fine pen-and-ink contours, and spontaneous gestural sketch details.
Museum-quality contemporary fine art aesthetic blending modern impressionism with luxury fashion illustration. Faces rendered with high realism and luminous skin tones, while surrounding forms dissolve into painterly abstraction, sketch fragments, and unfinished artistic marks. Dynamic contrast between bold impasto textures and delicate hand-drawn lines. Organic integration of watercolor stains, cross-hatched shading, loose construction sketches, and atmospheric paint splashes.
Soft muted color palette combining dusty mauve, warm beige, muted sage green, pale peach, faded terracotta, creamy ivory, soft taupe, cool gray-blue, and subtle lavender undertones. Luminous diffused lighting, delicate glow effects, broken-color technique, elegant tonal transitions, and painterly motion throughout. Backgrounds merge abstract paint fields, architectural sketch elements, urban line drawings, watercolor blooms, and expressive negative space. Sophisticated balance between finished realism and intentionally unfinished artistic process, conveying handcrafted authenticity, poetic visual storytelling, contemporary gallery elegance, and premium editorial illustration aesthetics."
````
