## Prompt / 提示词

````text
“Ultra-detailed 3D isometric miniature diorama of [COUNTRY] as a single floating map, divided into culturally distinct regions, connected by elegant winding roads.
Major regions and cities are represented using their most recognizable landmarks, architecture styles, landscapes, and environmental features appropriate to the country. Coastal areas include seas, cliffs, ports, and reflections. Mountain regions include elevation, forests, snow, or mist where relevant. Urban areas feature iconic buildings and structured layouts. Rural regions include plains, deserts, farmland, or valleys depending on geography.
Each region features unique textures, lighting, climate, colors, and cultural patterns inspired by local heritage and architecture.
Isometric perspective, miniature scale, floating landmass style.
Cinematic lighting, soft shadows, ultra-realistic textures.
High-end architectural visualization, storytelling composition.
Background: pure white.
Text: place the country name "COUNTRY" at the top-center in clean black typography.
No people, no labels, no extra text, no watermark.
1080×1080, ultra-detailed, premium aesthetic.”
````
