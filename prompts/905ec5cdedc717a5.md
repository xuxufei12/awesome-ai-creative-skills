## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Vertical top-down transformation ad. SUBJECT: a worker at a desk and a [brand] focus-gummy bottle. 0–2s (locked overhead, sickly green monitor glow flattening everything): a person slumped head-on-forearm beside toppled energy cans, fingers twitching toward a fourth, a low electrical buzz. 2–3s (interrupt — a hand enters and sweeps the can aside, placing the [brand] bottle, a warm highlight blooming on the cap): a single clean chime. 3–8s (slow crane-up, time-lapse cadence in two quick beats): the person straightens, the desk clears, the light shifts from green to clean daylight white, eyes alert, a steady pen now moving across paper. FIDELITY: the bottle keeps one consistent label, color, and shape; hands stay correctly numbered — no warping, text drift, duplicate hands, or label garble. SOUND: anxious buzz, the single chime, then a calm pen scratch.
````
