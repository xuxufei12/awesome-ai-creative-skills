A medium full shot studio portrait of [NAME] with an athletic build, leaning forward toward the camera in a dynamic, angled pose. She has a cheerful and confident expression, smiling warmly at eye level. She is wearing a sleeveless blue and white crop-top cheerleader uniform with white V-shaped stripes on the chest, a matching high-waisted pleated skirt in white with blue stripes along the hem, and tall white socks. Her right hand rests on her knee, while her left hand is on her hip, holding a vibrant blue pom-pom behind her. Her hair is styled with a small, matching black clip, and she wears black triangular geometric earrings complemented by light, natural makeup.
The scene is set against a clean, solid peach ice studio background. The environment features soft, bright, and diffused indoor studio lighting distributed evenly and flatteringly, creating delicate highlights on her skin and hair with minimal, soft shadows for a clean, professional aesthetic. Shot with a 50mm lens at f/4, ISO 100, and 1/250s shutter speed, ensuring crisp sharpness across the subject and props with moderate depth of field and a clean separation from the background.
