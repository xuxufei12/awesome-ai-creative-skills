## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Handheld phone selfie, woman wrapped in a chunky knit blanket on a couch at night, rain streaking the dark window behind her, a lit [brand] candle glowing on the side table. 0-2s: she half-whispers to the lens and tilts the candle so the flame and the melted wax pool catch the light, label clearly readable. 2-4s: slow push-in as she shuts her eyes and inhales over the rim, shoulders visibly dropping, a soft 'mmm.' 4-6s: she tugs the blanket higher, flicks her gaze to the rain, then back to camera with a sleepy, settled smile. Warm flickering candlelight as key, cool window-blue rim light, visible knit weave and a thin wisp of smoke rising. Candle jar holds consistent shape, label, and finish, no deformation, drift, or artifacts. Implied sound: rain ticking on glass, one contented sigh.
````
