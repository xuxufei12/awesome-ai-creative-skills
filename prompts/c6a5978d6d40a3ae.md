## Prompt / 提示词

````text
"A hyper-realistic, cinematic vertical split-screen portrait. The right side shows a high-resolution, sharp photograph of [Subject Name] in their youth with smooth skin and vibrant features. The left side shows the same person as an elderly individual, with deep wrinkles, age spots, and graying hair, perfectly aligned at the center. 8k resolution, detailed skin pores, dramatic studio lighting, neutral background, shot on 85mm lens."
Key Elements for this Style:
Composition: Terms like "vertical split-screen" or "bisected portrait" ensure the half-and-half look.
Alignment: Using the phrase "perfectly aligned at the center" helps the AI match the eyes and facial structure across both halves.
Texture: Including "detailed skin pores," "hyper-realistic," and "8k" is essential for the gritty, high-definition look seen in the reference."
````
