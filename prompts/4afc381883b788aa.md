## Prompt / 提示词

````text
PROMPT ⤵️
A contemporary expressive oil painting style with bold, dynamic brushwork and semi-abstract realism. Visible, confident brush strokes in layered patches and broken color blocks, blending figurative portraiture with painterly abstraction. Rich warm palette dominated by peach, golden beige, soft amber, rose pink, muted magenta, and deep burgundy, contrasted with subtle cool accents of olive green and desaturated teal. Loose, gestural application with thick and thin strokes interwoven, creating a sense of movement and spontaneity. Edges shift between soft blended transitions and sharp painterly marks. Light is warm and diffused, creating glowing skin tones with luminous highlights. Background and subject merge through color harmony and directional strokes, giving a slightly impressionistic, unfinished yet intentional aesthetic. Textural depth resembling oil on canvas, with visible layering and painterly surface variation.
````
