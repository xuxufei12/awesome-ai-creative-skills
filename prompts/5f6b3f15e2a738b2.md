## Prompt / 提示词

````text
Handheld near-POV clip, college-age guy sitting on concrete apartment stairs, [brand] shoebox balanced on his knees, late-afternoon sun raking in sideways. 0-2s: he flips the lid off and tilts the box toward the lens, tissue paper crinkling, a wide grin. 2-3s: fast snap-pan down to his hands lifting one sneaker out and rotating it so the stitching and outsole flare in the golden light. 3-5s: he slides it on and double-knots quickly, then taps the toe twice against the step. 5-8s: he stands and walks two paces away as the camera bobs after him, glancing back over his shoulder with a smirk. Gritty concrete texture, warm lens flare, slight handheld shake. Footwear holds consistent silhouette, logo placement, and finish, no deformation, drift, or artifacts. Implied sound: distant street traffic, paper crinkle, a single sole scuff.
````
