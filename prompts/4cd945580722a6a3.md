## Prompt / 提示词

````text
First-person optical reveal, 16:9. POV through fogged, smudged eyeglasses in dim indoor light — the room a blurred amber haze, fingerprints catching glare across the field. [0-2s] the blurry POV drifts and the focus hunts, soft and frustrating, edges bleeding. [2-3s] a hand lifts the old glasses out of frame and a rack-focus snaps hard. [3-6s] the same view returns through clean [brand] lenses — crystal sharp, anti-glare coating killing the reflections, edges crisp, color true and bright. [6-8s] a subtle parallax tilt brings the slim frame into the near foreground, light gliding cleanly along the coating with a single controlled glint. Frame shape, hinge, and lens curvature stay identical between states — no warping, ghosting, double-vision artifacts, or geometry drift. Implied sound: a soft cloth wipe, the click of frames settling onto the bridge.
````
