## Prompt / 提示词

````text
A serene skincare moment in a bright minimalist glass conservatory, leaves softly diffusing midday light. 0-2s: an extreme macro of a glass dropper as a single amber pearl of [product] serum swells at the tip, light refracting through it, then falls in slow motion onto an open palm and spreads. 2-4s: a smooth tilt-up to a woman with freckled, dewy skin pressing the serum along her cheekbone with two fingertips, eyes lowered, lips relaxed; potted greenery sits dreamily out of focus behind frosted glass. 4-6s: a slow lateral orbit as the frosted [brand] bottle rests on a pale stone ledge, fine condensation beading on it, a leaf shadow sliding across the label. The bottle keeps a consistent label, cap, and form throughout; no melting, warping, text drift, or artifacts. Audio: faint glass chimes, distant birdsong, one slow breath. Mood: clean, luminous, restorative. Palette: soft white, sage green, dewy neutral; high-key, airy.
````
