# diko0071/remotion-video-skills

A video factory where every product video is code. Built on Remotion: scenes are React components driven by frame math, so videos are deterministic, reviewable in pull requests, and reusable through a growing kit of motion blocks. It was built to ship Ryze AI's product videos at 30-50 a week; the kit, the camera physics and the measured-replica tooling are general.
