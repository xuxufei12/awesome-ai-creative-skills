# AmirSbss/spotlight

Open-source Agent Skill that turns a website, phone clips or a CSV into a finished short video: launch videos, promo reels and narrated explainers, made by Claude Code, Codex CLI or Hermes Agent. With music, captions in any language (Arabic and Persian too) and built-in quality checks.
