## 按目标搭配 / Choose a route

- **日常原理**：AuroraBarista 组织开头，gui-lin 或 aliangSSS 承担动画；观众先猜的镜头可参考 wchhywizd。 / Structure the hook, animate the mechanism, and invite a prediction.
- **历史人物纪录片**：sansheng-distill 整理材料 → history-story-video 拆分镜 → code-doc-film 表现路线与事件。 / Research → storyboard → geographic and event scenes.
- **诗人或人物视觉章节**：code-poem-film 表现作品，create-chinese-ink-video 做人物显影；这些片段需要接入完整的生平叙事。 / Use poem animation or ink reveals as chapters within a larger biography.
- **历史访谈**：historical-interview-scripts 提供虚构娱乐脚本，勿当真实引语。 / Fictional interviews belong to entertainment, not historical testimony.

以上搭配为本目录编辑建议，尚未联调。 / These combinations are editorial suggestions, not an integrated tested pipeline.
