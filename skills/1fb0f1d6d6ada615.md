# video-skit

Original AI comedy skits with a twist ending (10–15 s, recurring characters such as "Aju", the orange office-worker cat). Generates a ready-to-publish vertical video locally at zero cost - series title + realistic cute-pet footage + a deadpan newsreader-style voice-over + a yellow punchline caption + a twist at the end. Triggers - funny video, comedy skit, short skit, Aju, Aju at work, plot twist, funny pets, make something funny, make a funny one like this, a screenshot of a funny AI skit, /video-skit. For motivational quote + animal footage videos use video-quote instead.

# One line / a screenshot → original comedy skit with a twist (fully automatic)

Shares the same pipeline and the same QC as `video-quote` (motivational quotes); only **the writing is different**. General rules for generation, QC and publishing (steps 2–4 and troubleshooting) are in `video-quote`'s SKILL.md; this file covers only what is specific to skits.

All commands run from the repo root.

## Step 0: read the character card first

Always open `characters/aju-cat.md` (the current lead) with Read first. It has the persona, fixed elements, the **prompt lock blocks** (CAT / STYLE), the topic bank and the safety checklist. For a new character, create a new card in `characters/` in the same format and **get the user's approval before using it**.

**Casting principles**: prefer **animals and pets** (AI flaws are less visible, the look is easy to keep consistent, they don't need to talk, and compliance risk is lowest). Don't use humans in period costume (the local model can't keep the same face across 3 shots), and don't use uniforms or law-enforcement figures.

**Process red line: the user asked to "confirm before generating". After writing the script, send the user the plan table (shots, picture, narration, caption, twist, compliance check) and wait for "confirmed" before running generation — don't start automatically.**

| The user gives | What to do |
|---|---|
| A one-line theme (e.g. "an Aju episode about the year-end bonus") | Write it directly |
| A screenshot of someone else's comedy video | Learn only the **structure** (what contrast, where the twist is, how long); swap in our own subject and characters, **never touch the original's characters or lines** |
| Only "make something funny" | Pick an unused topic from the topic bank in `characters/aju-cat.md` (check which episodes already exist in `scripts/`) |

## Step 1: write the script `scripts/epNN.json`

Follow the format of `scripts/ep04.json` (Aju episode 1, "The Raise"). Top-level fields:

```json
{
  "style": "skit",
  "title": "Aju at Work\nEp. N: <two to four words>",
  "title_size": 72,
  "voice": "en-US-AndrewNeural", "rate": "+0%",
  "cta": "Follow Aju · back to work next episode",
  "scenes": [ ... 3 shots ... ]
}
```

**Comedy structure (always use this)**:
1. **Shot 1 · hook + setup**: the very first line states the situation or contrast ("Aju has worked overtime seven days straight."). No background intro first
2. **Shot 2 · escalation**: push the expectation to its peak ("Today, the boss finally announced a raise."), with a `pause` of 0.8–1.0 s
3. **Shot 3 · twist**: smash the expectation with a tiny number or object ("Size of the raise: one kibble." + caption "+1 kibble"), with `pause` around 1.5 s so viewers can finish laughing. Most effective twist types: let-down expectation + number contrast > office-worker relatability > cute-pet contrast > deadpan nonsense. "It was him all along" identity twists are easy to guess — use sparingly; puns only as garnish

- Setup takes 60–70 %, punchline + reaction 30–40 %; total length 10–15 s
- The "rule of three" also works: two normal beats, the third absurd
- The punchline goes into `caption` (big yellow caption, **keep it very short — a few words**; `caption_at` is the second it pops up). The narrator stays deadpan and never laughs first
- Each episode has one question viewers can answer in the comments, written into the post ("Is your office like this too?")
- **Voice-over**: one fixed narrator voice (the original Chinese series used `zh-CN-YunxiNeural`, chosen by the user from three versions; for English use `en-US-AndrewNeural`). **Tune `rate` / `pitch` / `volume` per line to the emotion** — a flat read sounds stiff: setup lines tired or calm (`-8%` to `-15%`, `-2` to `-6Hz`) → anticipation excited (`+6%`, `+12` to `+14Hz`, `volume +10%`) → twist deflated and slower (`-22%` to `-25%`, `-12` to `-14Hz`), with "…" as a pause in the twist line ("Size of the raise… one kibble."). Set `"caption_at": "beat"` to sync the caption to the reveal word automatically, or `"end"` to pop it when the line finishes. Add `"push": 0.12` on the twist shot for a slow push-in. For a second character, override `"voice"` in that scene (e.g. `en-US-AvaNeural`, `en-US-BrianNeural`)
- **Shots without a line**: `"text": ""` plus `"seconds": 2.0` — picture plus caption only, good for a "silent reaction shot"
- **Pronunciation**: same rule as `video-quote` — reword anything the TTS voice reads wrongly

**Writing `prompt_en`**: `A <CAT lock block, verbatim> <one simple action>, <setting>. <STYLE lock block, verbatim>`.
- The lock blocks must be **copied word for word** so the character doesn't drift between shots
- Any notices, signs or scrolls in the picture are always `blank` (the model can't render text well); add text later with `caption`
- Don't ask for open-mouth talking; shot 3 is usually a close-up revealing the detail
- Give every shot `"seed": 2024`; change it only if the picture is bad

**Originality and compliance red lines**:
- No characters from novels, films, TV, anime or games; no real historical figures, no real people's faces or voices
- Nothing vulgar, gross or shocking (vomit, excrement, blood); no police, military, doctors, city-inspector or other uniforms; don't mock any profession
- Animals are never hit, hurt or shown frightened and struggling; no children; no fire, knives, heights or other dangerous actions; no ghosts or fortune-telling
- No real brands or trademarks; don't imitate real news programmes' logos or anchors
- Every episode is a new joke — never mass-post the same template with only the subtitles changed
- **No** "AI" text on the picture (the user's request); at publish time **always** tick "Content is AI-generated" — Douyin then adds its own AI label to the video (China's Measures for Labeling AI-Generated Synthetic Content require an explicit label; missing it risks throttling and penalties). Remind the user on every delivery, and write "The story is fictional" in the post description

Once written, show the user the title, each shot's line and caption (and remind them they can stop it at any time), then wait for the confirmation described above.

## Steps 2–4: generate, QC, publish

Exactly as steps 2–4 of `video-quote`, same commands:

```bash
.venv/bin/python make_video.py scripts/epNN.json --provider local-video
.venv/bin/python check_video.py scripts/epNN.json
```

- 3 shots take about 10 minutes (about 3 minutes each, with a 1-minute cool-down between)
- **Smooth mode (enabled automatically by `style: skit`; the user compared versions and preferred it)**: each shot generates only the seconds it needs (24 fps, 49–89 frames), **with no frame interpolation** — every frame is a real model frame. At compose time it applies **de-flicker (hqdn3d) + motion interpolation to 60 fps (minterpolate)**, so the final video is 60 fps (the user compared A native 24 / B de-flicker / C de-flicker + interpolation and chose C; 30 or 48 fps are avoided because of duplicate frames and uneven platform transcoding). Shots hard-cut with no black flash. Composing takes about 1 minute of CPU per video. During QC, look at a few interpolated in-between frames for warping or ghosting. Because of this, keep each shot (line + pause) **under 3.7 s**; longer shots get slowed down or looped
- Don't use `--decode-backend wan-vae`: on this 24 GB Mac it runs out of memory even at 73 frames (tested)
- In QC, **also check character consistency**: hat, whiskers and clothing colours must match across the 3 shots; re-roll a shot that differs too much with a new `seed` (at most 2 times)
- Check that the caption doesn't overflow the frame or cover the character's face (if it does, adjust `caption_at` or shorten the caption)

**Skit post template** (write into `works/<code>_<name>/epNN_post.txt`, same field order as `video-quote`):
- [Title] `Aju at Work · Ep. N: <one contrast hook>`
- [Description] a one-line synopsis (don't spoil the twist) + a comment question + "The story is fictional; this video contains AI-generated content" + hashtags such as `#AIskit #officelife #orangecat #plottwist #funnypets` (swap 1–2 for the episode's topic, e.g. #bonus #slackingoff)
- [Declaration] content is AI-generated
- Background music: pick something light, quirky or "deadpan serious" from the in-app library (search for comedic or playful instrumental music), music volume 15–25 %
- Collection: "Aju at Work" — add every episode so viewers can binge (helps followers grow)
