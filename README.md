# Awesome AI Creative Skills

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

> Skills, prompts, tools and examples that turn ideas into videos, images, motion graphics and 3D worlds.

[![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re) [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/xuxufei12/awesome-ai-creative-skills?style=flat)](https://github.com/xuxufei12/awesome-ai-creative-skills/stargazers)

Explore ways to create imaginative images, videos, motion graphics and 3D scenes with AI. This collection brings together prompts, reusable Skills and examples of what they can make—from a playful image to a complete animation, with inspiration and practical starting points for your own creations.

## New · Make a style your own

Lemo-Opuscar now offers **59 film styles**, including **8 new editing and documentary styles**. Adapt a library style or design your own, preview it, and save it for your next film.

The creator’s example keeps the Swiss grid, beat and typography, turning flat shapes into clay, felt, glass and chrome objects. **From above it reads as a poster; tilt the camera and it becomes a 3D world.**

## How to use

1. Pick a category, then open a project, Skill or prompt.
2. Check the original source, license, dependencies and required inputs. Host labels reflect upstream mentions only.
3. Start with still frames or a 5–10 second draft before rendering a complete piece.
4. Results belong to upstream creators. Missing text, media or reproduction evidence is explicitly labelled.
